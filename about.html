Garrett Collier

Professional Background

Born and raised in Hot Springs and proud graduate of Hot Springs High School, Garrett Collier is running for City Director for District 5 to strengthen communication between our city and county, improve public safety, and ensure transparency and communication in local government.

With a Master's in Public Administration and experience in the City's Planning Commission, Board of Zoning and Adjustments, and now Art Advisory Committee, Garrett has seen firsthand how collaboration and thoughtful leadership can move our community forward.

His campaign focuses on practical solutions for Hot Springs — not politics — centered on transparency, accountability, and putting people first.

About Garrett Collier

Garrett Collier is a lifelong Hot Springs resident running for City Director in District 5. His campaign focuses on transparency, collaboration, and community growth that benefits every resident.
